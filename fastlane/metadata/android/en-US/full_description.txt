CricZen is a free, open-source Android application that provides live cricket scores, match updates, and news. 

Features:
- Real-time live scores and commentary
- Upcoming match schedules and series information
- Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mode for floating scores
- Offline caching for recently viewed matches
- Clean, Material 3 design without ads or trackers

Perfect for cricket fans who want a fast, privacy-respecting, and lightweight app to follow their favorite teams.
